### Task 1: EI fixed-rate positions/orientations/dc engine

**Files:** extend `crates/axilog-core/src/analysis/replay.rs` (or a sibling `ei_replay.rs`);
tests `crates/axilog-core/tests/ei_replay_golden.rs`.

**Requirements:** verify GW2EI's polled-position algorithm (fixed pollingRate grid — confirm
300ms and whether it's constant or fight-length-dependent; linear interpolation between raw
position events; extrapolation/clamping at track edges; when a player has NO position events)
and its orientation polling (facing sc=21 → degrees; verify EI's angle convention + rounding)
and dc intervals (i64 sentinel bracketing observed on the reference: `[[i64::MIN, start],
[end, i64::MAX]]` — verify GW2EI's exact rule incl. mid-fight disconnects). Implement as a
pure function over the raw events + M9's per-map transform, producing per-player
`{ start, end, positions: Vec<[f64;2]>, orientations: Vec<f64>, dc: Vec<[i64;2]> }` in MAP
PIXELS on the EI grid. Rounding to EI's precision (reference shows 3 decimals — verify).
Calibrate vs the local post-era EI JSON: start/end/dc EXACT; positions ≥99% within 1 pixel
(count+document outliers, M9 precedent 99.77%); orientations documented tolerance. Unit tests:
interpolation between two known points, edge clamping, empty-track player, dc bracketing.
Real-log sanity both eras.
